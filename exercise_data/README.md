# Exercise data

Data for each phase of the exercise is added here as the phase starts.

## Phase 1 (25 October)

An outbreak of a new measles variant, mostly in Denden around Vejager, with one
case in Sweway. Folder: [`phase_1/`](phase_1/)

- [`Phase 1 handout.pdf`](phase_1/Phase%201%20handout.pdf): the situation, the data and the tasks.
- [`line_list_phase1_2026-10-25.csv`](phase_1/line_list_phase1_2026-10-25.csv):
  the 68 cases recorded up to 25 October, with test and onset dates, country,
  region, age, vaccination status, hospital admission and discharge dates, and
  deaths.
- [`travel_between_countries_one_way.csv`](phase_1/travel_between_countries_one_way.csv):
  estimated trips per day in each direction between each pair of countries.

The demography of each country is bundled with the package: see
[`docs/data.md`](../docs/data.md).

## Phase 1b (1 November)

New data, reported on 1 November. Folder: [`phase_1b/`](phase_1b/)

- [`CT data explainer.pdf`](phase_1b/CT%20data%20explainer.pdf): how the contact
  investigation was done, and what each column means.
- [`contact_investigation_2026-11-01.csv`](phase_1b/contact_investigation_2026-11-01.csv):
  the 66 contacts of the first 30 cases in Denden who tested positive, with or
  without symptoms.
- [`line_list_inject.csv`](phase_1b/line_list_inject.csv): the line list updated
  to 1 November, with 136 cases (132 in Denden, 4 in Sweway).

## Phase 2 (10 January 2027)

The outbreak has grown in all four countries, and teams are asked to assess
interventions. Folder: [`phase_2/`](phase_2/)

- [`Phase 2 handout.pdf`](phase_2/Phase%202%20handout.pdf): the situation, the
  interventions under consideration and the tasks.
- [`line_list_phase2_2027-01-10.csv`](phase_2/line_list_phase2_2027-01-10.csv):
  the line list updated to 10 January, with 62,011 cases.
- [`serosurvey_explainer.pdf`](phase_2/serosurvey_explainer.pdf): how the
  serosurvey was done, and what each column means.
- [`serosurvey_denden_D2_2026-12.csv`](phase_2/serosurvey_denden_D2_2026-12.csv):
  a serosurvey of recent infection in region D2 of Denden, 14 to 18 December,
  by 10-year age group.
- [`contacts_by_age.csv`](phase_2/contacts_by_age.csv): a contact matrix by
  10-year age group, from a contact survey across the region.
